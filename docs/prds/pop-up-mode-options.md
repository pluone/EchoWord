### pop-up mode options

在选项页中增加以下选项，需要判断系统类型

#### 在 mac 系统中支持以下选项

Pop-up mode
open by hovering over a word/phrase or after a click （no need to select） | 即通过 hover 或者点击
open only by hovering over a word/phrase | 通过 hover
open only after a click or tap on a word/phrase  | 通过点击或者按压
open only after an option+click on a word/phrase  | 通过 option+click
open only after a command+click on a word/phrase  | 通过 command+click
open only after a press on a command key while hovering  | hover 之后按 command 键
open only after a press on a control key while hovering  | hover 之后按 control 键
disable pop-up | 禁用

#### 在 Windows 系统中支持以下选项

open by hovering over a word/phrase or after a click （no need to select）| 通过hover 或者点击
open only by hovering over a word/phrase | 通过 hover
open only after a click or tap on a word/phrase | 通过点击或者按压
open only after an alt+cick on a word/phrase | 通过 alt+click
open only after a ctrl +click on a word/phrase | 通过 ctrl+click
open only after a press on a ctrl key while hovering | hover 之后按 ctrl 键
disable pop-up | 禁用

默认选项是通过 hover 或者鼠标点击，当前代码中仅实现了 hover，因此需要增加其他的所有选项。
